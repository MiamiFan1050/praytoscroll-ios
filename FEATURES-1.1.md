# Pray to Scroll 1.1: plan (branch `v1.1`; `main` stays at 44f8b8f for build 5)

1. **Real lock (headline, free forever, 4.10).** FamilyControls `.individual` → Apple's FamilyActivityPicker (TikTok, Instagram… with real icons) → ManagedSettings shields. The shield reads "Pray to open TikTok" in our look. Its Pray button posts a notification that opens our prayer (Apple lets a shield do no more). Amen lifts the shield for your window. A DeviceActivity monitor re-shields when the window ends, even if you're still inside the app.
2. **Every escape is shield-aware.** The emergency pass (5 min, once a day), pause (1/3/7 days) and the Daily key (until 4 AM) all unshield and relock on time. Apple's 15-minute minimum schedule is met by back-dating the interval start. That needs a device check, as do the reliability cases (§4.4): the shield always offers a way to pray, and deleting the app never strands you.
3. **Onboarding + Home make the premise obvious.** "Choose the apps that wait for your Amen" opens the picker as an onboarding step (suggest 1–3 apps). Home shows "Locked: [real icons]", or "Open for 12 min". If authorization is denied, it falls back to today's Shortcuts gate. The Shortcuts guide only appears in that case.
4. **Tested without a phone.** A `LockEngine` protocol puts the real shield store and a fake behind one API. Unit tests cover lock, then Amen, then relock; emergency, pause and the Daily key; and denied authorization, then the Shortcuts fallback. UI tests drive the onboarding picker step through a Debug mock (the simulator can't show Apple's picker). The crawl stays lean.
5. **Signing (I create nothing).** Four bundle IDs get `com.apple.developer.family-controls` plus the app group: com.amenkeyapp.ios, .shieldconfig, .shieldaction and .monitor. That means 4 new App Store provisioning profiles with Family Controls (Distribution), after Apple approves S7N7QC6BD5. Each extension gets its own PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy.
6. **Scroll the Word feels like the best scroll apps.** Topic feeds (anxiety, peace, strength, love, gratitude… from the 14 themes already tagged on all 366 verses). A one-line "what this means" per verse, written by us. A double-tap heart to save. Swipe left to pray the verse. No repeats across sessions (a persisted shuffle bag).
7. **Comfort and reach.** A−/A+ text size, the #1 complaint from older users (§4.6). Read aloud with the system's enhanced voices, default off (§3). A KJV/WEB toggle; both are public domain, nothing licensed (§7.16). A beautiful verse image card to share.
8. **More ways to pray.** Topics: family, friends, healing, anxiety, work, gratitude, forgiveness, guidance, morning, night. Each gets ≥40 original prompts in a no-repeat shuffle bag, so 5–15 gates a day don't repeat (§4.5, §7.9). The prayer list and journal get "Answered ✓", a history, and an "God answered" share card (§7.14).
9. **Pray for a friend in two taps.** Pick a person or a contact name, pray, then "Let them know" with an editable text or an image card. Circles get **prayer requests**: a member posts a request (topic + 140 filtered characters), others tap "I prayed 🙏", and the count shows. Report, block, the name/text filter and no DMs stay (1.2). This needs one new CloudKit record type that Mark deploys.
10. **Research items still missing from v1:** streak backup to iCloud + "I prayed offline yesterday" (2 a month) + an optional rest day (§7.10). "Advent 25" circle challenge (§7.18): Advent starts Nov 29, likely before 1.1 ships, so it's ready the day it does. Order of delivery: (b) lock → (c) scroll → (d) prayer, with a report after each.
