How it works

Your apps wait for you to pray.

Set it up once in about a minute with Apple's Shortcuts app. Pray to Scroll shows you every tap.

  1. Choose your apps

    Pick the ones that pull you in: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, Snapchat, Facebook, Reddit, Threads, Pinterest, or your own.

  2. Open one, pray first

    When you open a gated app, Pray to Scroll steps in with a short prayer: 30 seconds, a guided minute, a verse, or a friend who needs it.

  3. Say Amen, open your app

    Press and hold Amen. Your app opens and the gate stays quiet for 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. You decide.

Pray to Scroll works through an automation you create in Apple's Shortcuts app. It pauses your apps with a prayer rather than locking them at the system level, and you can turn it off anytime. Once a day, an emergency pass lets you straight through.

The Prayer Gate

A calm, full-screen moment before the feed: a slow breathing glow, a prompt, and a press-and-hold Amen that unlocks your app. Choose a quick prayer, a guided ACTS prayer, or today's verse to pray through.

Pray for someone

Unlock by praying for a person on your list, or anyone in your contacts. Pray to Scroll suggests whoever you haven't prayed for in a while, then offers to let them know.

Let Sarah know? “I just prayed for you 🙏”

Streaks and answered prayers

Watch your streak grow, with one freeze a week for the hard days. Milestones, a calendar of faithfulness, and an Answered Prayers wall to look back on when you need it most.

The math
30days

Two hours of scrolling a day adds up to about thirty days a year. Pray to Scroll doesn't ask you to quit. It asks for a minute with God first, every time.
