The Prayer Gate

How does the Prayer Gate work?

You create a personal automation in Apple's Shortcuts app: “When Instagram is opened, run Pray to Scroll › Pray First.” From then on:

  1. You open Instagram (or any app you've gated).
  2. Shortcuts runs Pray to Scroll's Pray First action. If you're still inside your unlock window, it does nothing and you carry on.
  3. If not, Pray to Scroll opens on the prayer screen. Pray, then press and hold Amen.
  4. Tap Continue to Instagram. The gate stays quiet for the window you chose (5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes).
Does Pray to Scroll use Screen Time? Does it block apps?

Not in this version. Pray to Scroll's lock is the Prayer Gate: a Shortcuts automation you control that opens Pray to Scroll, and a prayer, before your app. It doesn't lock apps at the system level, and you can turn the automation off at any time. We're working on an optional, stricter mode built on Apple's Screen Time. It isn't available yet.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, email or password. Everything works on your iPhone. Prayer Circles use your iCloud sign-in behind the scenes, and we never see your Apple ID.

Can I pray without setting up the gate?

Yes. Tap Pray now on the Home screen any time, or use the “Pray now” widget. Your streak and stats count either way.

Setting up the Shortcuts automation

How do I set up the automation, step by step?

It takes about a minute per app. Pray to Scroll shows the same steps in the app (Settings → Gated apps), with an Open Shortcuts button.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap the Automation tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap + in the top corner (or New Automation if this is your first one).
  3. Scroll down and choose App.
  4. Tap Choose, select the app you want to gate (for example Instagram), and tap Done.
  5. Make sure Is Opened is ticked and Is Closed is not.
  6. Select Run Immediately. You can also turn off Notify When Run. Tap Next.
  7. Tap New Blank Automation, then Add Action.
  8. Search for Pray to Scroll and choose Pray First.
  9. Tap the app field in the action and pick the same app, so Pray to Scroll knows where to send you after you pray. Tap Done.
  10. Open the app once to test it. Pray to Scroll should appear, and the app will show as Protected ✓ in Pray to Scroll.

Gating more than one app? Repeat these steps once for each app, so every automation sends you back to the right place after you pray.

Can I gate an app that isn't on Pray to Scroll's list?

Yes. Add it as a custom app in Pray to Scroll with its URL scheme (so “Continue” can open it), then create the automation above for that app in Shortcuts.

Troubleshooting

The automation doesn't fire. What should I check?
  1. Are you inside your unlock window? After you pray, the gate stays quiet on purpose for 5–60 minutes. The Home screen shows how much time is left.
  2. Is the automation turned on? In Shortcuts → Automation, open it and check it isn't disabled.
  3. Is it set to Run Immediately? “Run After Confirmation” waits for you to tap a notification first.
  4. Is “Is Opened” ticked for the right app?
  5. Is the action Pray to Scroll's Pray First? If it shows an error or a blank action, delete the action and add it again.
  6. Did you update iOS or reinstall Pray to Scroll recently? Open Pray to Scroll once, then check the automation still lists Pray First. If not, recreate it.
  7. Still nothing? Restart your iPhone, then delete the automation and create it again using the steps above.

Once the gate has run for an app, Pray to Scroll marks it Protected ✓ in Settings → Gated apps, so you can see at a glance which ones are working.

“Pray First” doesn't appear when I search in Shortcuts.

Open Pray to Scroll once (Shortcuts learns about an app's actions after it has been opened), then search again for “Pray to Scroll”. If it still doesn't show, restart your iPhone.

A banner says “Running your automation” every time.

That's iOS telling you the automation ran. Open the automation in Shortcuts and turn off Notify When Run.

“Continue to [app]” doesn't open my app.

Check the app is installed and that the Pray First action in your automation has the matching app selected. For a custom app, check its URL scheme in Pray to Scroll.

Using Pray to Scroll

How do I change the unlock window?

Open Pray to Scroll → Settings → Unlock window, and choose 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes. The new window applies from your next prayer.

How do I change how I pray to unlock?

Settings → Unlock style: Quick prayer (30 seconds), Guided (1–3 minutes through Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving and Supplication), Verse + prayer, or Pray for someone.

What is Scroll the Word?

A full-screen feed of Bible verses that you swipe through one at a time, like a short-video app. It's the healthy thing to scroll after you pray. Verses come from the World English Bible, built into the app.

What is the emergency pass?

Once a day, tap I need it now on the prayer screen to open your app for 5 minutes without praying. It's counted honestly in your stats, and it resets each day.

How do streaks and streak freezes work?

Pray at least once a day to keep your streak. You get one streak freeze each week to cover a missed day. Milestones arrive at 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 50, 100 and 365 days.

Prayer Circles

How do Prayer Circles work?

A circle is a small group of friends who encourage each other. Create one in the Circles tab and Pray to Scroll gives you a six-character code and an invite link to share. Friends join with the code or the link. Each week, the circle shows everyone's prayer count and streak, and the count starts fresh the next week.

What can other members see about me?

Only the nickname you choose, your weekly prayer count and your streak. Never your journal, prayer list, requests or the names of people you pray for.

Circles say I need iCloud.

Circles use Apple's CloudKit, so you need to be signed in to iCloud in the Settings app. Everything else in Pray to Scroll works without iCloud.

How do I leave a circle?

Open the circle and tap Leave circle. Your membership record is deleted.

Your data

How do I delete my data?
  • Everything on your device: Settings → Reset all data, or delete the app.
  • Circle records: leave each circle first to delete your record right away. Otherwise circle records expire on their own, or contact us with your circle code and nickname and we'll remove it.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Which Bible translation does Pray to Scroll use?

The World English Bible (WEB), a modern English translation in the public domain.
