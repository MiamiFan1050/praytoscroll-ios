Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. This policy covers the Pray to Scroll iPhone app, its widgets and Shortcuts actions, and this website.

The short version

  • Your prayer list, journal, streaks, gate settings and everything else you do in Pray to Scroll are stored on your iPhone. We can't see them.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. We never sell or share your data.
  • Prayer Circles are optional. If you join one, it stores only a nickname, the circle code, your weekly prayer count and your streak in Apple's CloudKit, and nothing else.

Who we are

Pray to Scroll is made by Mark Morrison, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about your privacy, contact us.

What stays on your device

Pray to Scroll is built to work without sending your information anywhere. The following is stored only on your iPhone, using Apple's SwiftData and an App Group container that is shared only with Pray to Scroll's own widgets and Shortcuts actions:

  • Prayer Gate state: the apps you chose to gate, your unlock style and window, when your current unlock ends, when each gate last ran (so the app can show “Protected”), and your daily emergency pass use.
  • Prayer list: the people you pray for, their relationship to you, their avatar, your requests, answered prayers and “last prayed” dates.
  • Journal: your entries and anything they link to.
  • Streaks, stats and challenges: your prayer sessions, streaks, streak freezes, milestones and challenge progress.
  • Settings: the first name you enter (used only to greet you), reminder times and other preferences.

We have no server that receives this data, and no way to read it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, iOS may include this data in that backup. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we can't access them.

Prayer Circles (optional)

Prayer Circles let you and your friends encourage each other on a weekly leaderboard. They use Apple's CloudKit public database (container iCloud.com.amenkeyapp.ios). When you create or join a circle, Pray to Scroll stores:

StoredWhyWho can see it
The nickname you chooseSo friends know who's whoMembers of your circle

Your current streakThe leaderboardMembers of your circle

Nothing else is shared. Not your journal, your prayer list, the names of people you pray for, your requests, your contacts, your location or your real name (unless you choose to use it as your nickname).

CloudKit needs you to be signed in to iCloud. Apple links your circle record to an anonymous, app-specific identifier so only you can update it. We never receive your Apple ID, name, email address or phone number. Circle records are sent over encrypted connections and stored on Apple's servers. As the developer, we can view and delete circle records in Apple's CloudKit tools, and we do so only to provide support or remove records that break our Terms.

Removing circle data: leave the circle in the app and your membership record is deleted. Circle records that stop being updated also expire and are deleted automatically. You can also contact us with your circle code and nickname and we'll delete your record by hand.

Contacts

When you pray for someone from your contacts, iOS shows Apple's contact picker. Pray to Scroll never gets access to your address book. It uses only the name of the person you pick, and stores that name on your device in your prayer list.

Messages and sharing

After praying for someone, Pray to Scroll can open Apple's Messages sheet with a short message such as “I just prayed for you 🙏” and a link to the app. You can edit it, and you decide whether to send it from Apple's Messages. Pray to Scroll doesn't read your conversations or send anything on your behalf. Share cards and invite links go through Apple's share sheet in the same way.

Notifications, Shortcuts and Bible text

  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. We don't run a push server.
  • The Prayer Gate runs in Apple's Shortcuts app on your iPhone. Pray to Scroll records on your device which gated app triggered it and when, and nothing leaves your device.
  • Bible verses, including the Scroll the Word feed, come from the World English Bible, which is in the public domain, and are built into the app. Reading or scrolling them sends nothing anywhere.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. That sharing is controlled by you and covered by Apple's privacy policy.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, external fonts or third-party scripts. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses to run and secure its service, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. The circle invite page reads the code in its link inside your browser only.

When you contact us

Our contact form is hosted by Google Forms. We receive only what you type into it. If you leave an email address so we can reply, we use it only to answer you, and we delete messages once they're no longer needed. Google's privacy policy covers how Google runs the form.

Children

Pray to Scroll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has joined a Prayer Circle, contact us and we'll delete the record.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Reset all data erases everything stored on your device.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data from your iPhone, including its widgets' data.
  • Circle data: leave your circles first to remove your circle records right away. Otherwise they expire on their own, or you can contact us to remove them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. Because almost everything stays on your device, you control it directly in the app. For circle records, contact us and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. If a change affects what data Pray to Scroll handles, we'll say so in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
