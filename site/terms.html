1. What Pray to Scroll is

Pray to Scroll is an iPhone app, made by Mark Morrison (“we”, “us”), that helps you build a habit of praying before you scroll. It's free to use and doesn't need an account.

2. How the Prayer Gate works, and its limits

The Prayer Gate runs through an automation you create in Apple's Shortcuts app. When you open an app you've chosen, the automation opens Pray to Scroll first so you can pray.
