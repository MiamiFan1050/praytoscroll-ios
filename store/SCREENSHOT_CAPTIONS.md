# Pray to Scroll App Store screenshot captions

There are eight captions, in upload order, each six words or fewer. `frame.py` reads the table below.
The raw screenshot named `01-….png` gets caption 1, `02-….png` gets caption 2, and so on. Files
without a number prefix are matched in sorted order. Words between `*asterisks*` render in gold
italic. The optional Badge goes in a small gold pill above the caption: screenshot 1 carries the
category's strongest promise. Capture raws in `-demo` mode on the iPhone 6.9" simulator (1320×2868).

| # | Caption | Badge | Screen to capture |
|---|---------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Knees before *feeds.* | Free · No ads · No account · No tracking | Prayer session: breathing glow, timer ring, prompt, "Hold to say Amen" |
| 2 | Say *Amen* to unlock. | - | Celebration after Amen: streak +1, "Unlocked for 15 min", "Continue to Instagram" |
| 3 | Then scroll *the Word.* | - | Scroll the Word: full-screen verse card mid-swipe |
| 4 | Pray for someone. *Tell them.* | - | Pray for someone, ending on "Let Sarah know?" |
| 5 | Keep your *prayer streak* alive. | - | Streaks + stats: calendar heatmap, milestones, totals |
| 6 | Pray together in *Prayer Circles.* | - | Circle weekly leaderboard (demo circle of 6) |
| 7 | Remember every *answered prayer.* | - | Answered Prayers wall |
| 8 | Set up in *about a minute.* | - | Prayer Gate setup guide with numbered steps and "Protected ✓" checklist |

Caption word counts: 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5, 4, 5. Slogan "Knees before feeds." leads; the mascot lamb can sit in any raw screen that shows it.
