Knees before feeds.

Pray to Scroll locks your most distracting apps until you pray. Open Instagram and it's locked: tap Pray now, take a breath, pray, then press and hold Amen. Your apps open for as long as you chose, then lock again by themselves. Pray first. Scroll after.

HOW THE LOCK WORKS
• Pick the apps, categories and sites that pull you in from Apple's own Screen Time list. Apple gives Pray to Scroll anonymous tokens only: it never learns which apps you picked or how you use them.
• A locked app shows "Pray to open". Tap Pray now, then the notification, and pray.
• Say Amen and your apps open for your window: 15 minutes to 3 hours, a custom time, or the rest of the day. When it's up they lock again by themselves, even mid-scroll.
• Home always shows the lock's state: locked, open and when it locks again, or paused with a one-tap fix.
• Need it now? Once a day, an emergency pass opens your apps for 15 minutes without praying, counted honestly in your stats.
• Away for a few days? Pause the lock for a day, three days or a week. It comes back by itself.
• Prefer not to use Screen Time? The Shortcuts Prayer Gate is still there.

SCROLL THE WORD
After you pray, scroll something good. Scroll the Word is a full-screen feed of Bible verses you swipe through one at a time, just like the apps you know, except it leaves you fuller, not emptier. It's the healthy thing to scroll.

FOUR WAYS TO PRAY
• Quick prayer: 30 seconds, one prompt, Amen.
• Guided: 30 seconds to 5 minutes through Adoration, Confession, Thanksgiving and Supplication.
• Verse + prayer: read today's verse one line at a time, then pray it.
• Pray for someone: choose a person from your prayer list or your contacts, pray for them, then send "I just prayed for you" if you'd like.
Or unlock by reading: one verse with a line on what it means, or a 60 to 90 second devotional. Every one counts toward your streak.

PEOPLE AND ANSWERED PRAYERS
Keep a list of the people you love and what they're facing. Pray to Scroll suggests whoever you haven't prayed for in a while. When God answers, mark the request answered and it moves to your Answered Prayers wall, a record of faithfulness to come back to.

A HABIT THAT GROWS
• A daily streak, with one free streak freeze every week.
• Milestones at 3, 7, 14, 21, 30, 50, 100 and 365 days.
• A calendar of your prayers, minutes prayed, people prayed for, and every time you prayed instead of scrolling.
• Challenges: 7-day Fresh Start, 21-day Habit, 40-day Social Media Fast and Pray for 30 People.

PRAYER CIRCLES
Invite friends with a six-character code and encourage each other on a weekly board of prayers and streaks. There's no chat and no profile. A circle sees only the nickname and emoji you choose, your weekly prayer count and your streak.

ALSO INSIDE
• A verse for every day of the year from the World English Bible
• A private prayer journal with search
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Gentle reminders, including a heads-up when your streak is at risk
• Beautiful share cards for milestones and verses

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your prayer list, journal, streaks, locked apps and settings stay on your iPhone. There's no account to create, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. Prayer Circles are optional: if you join one, only your nickname, emoji, weekly prayer count and streak are shared, through Apple's iCloud.

FREE, FOR REAL
Every feature in Pray to Scroll is free. No trial, no paywall, and no paying to pray.

A note on how it works: the lock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) on your own iPhone. You choose what's locked, you can change it any time, and turning the Prayer Gate off in Settings unlocks everything at once.
