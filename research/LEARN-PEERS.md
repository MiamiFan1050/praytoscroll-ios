# Pray to Scroll "learn the Bible" layer: peer research (read 2026-11-19)

Scope: 30 apps that teach the Bible, quiz it, help memorize it, or feed it TikTok-style, plus Duolingo and Wordle as the mechanic sources. The prayer-lock apps are in COMPETITORS.md and aren't repeated here.
Data comes from the US iTunes Search/Lookup API (22 search terms, today's ratings), US App Store pages (in-app purchase lists), the review RSS (the 50–150 most recent reviews per app, 22 apps), developer pages, and Duolingo's blog. "≤2★ x/y" means x of the y most recent reviews were 1–2 stars. Quotes are verbatim. YouVersion's help center didn't show up in search, so its streak rules come from copies of its announcement. Screensdesign gives conflicting numbers for Manna (see Sources).

Big surprises, by rating count: a word-game collection app (Oakever, 724,764 ratings) out-rates every learn app. A plain KJV Verse-of-the-Day reader (Ozion, 499,471) and AI-chat Bible Chat (375,639) come next. No Duolingo-style Bible app has passed 33K ratings.

## 2. Patterns

- **The sticky ones pair one thing that's the same for everyone each day with a count.** YouVersion pairs VOTD with a streak, Versle a daily verse with win streaks, UniRise a Daily Challenge, Bible Loop a daily verse game with a crown. Versle fans share scores with family and friends every morning.
- **The free ones get the fewest low-star reviews.** YouVersion has 0 ≤2★ of its 150 most recent, Versify 2/150, Bible Loop 3/76. The paid feeds do worst: Bible Shorts 89/121, Scroll The Bible 46/150, BibleWatch 36/150. Ascend is the worst learn app at 35/150. The words "paying for scripture", "Not free" and "greed" come up again and again.
- **Duolingo clones copy the punishments too, and get blamed for them.** Manna's hearts lock you out for 5 hours. Ascend sells unlimited energy. Shepherd's lamb faints. Versle capped Blitz at once every 12 hours, and that cap drew 1★ reviews from loyal users.
- **Quiz apps lose trust over answers.** Disputed counts ("88 books"), a wrong book (Proverbs vs Psalms broke a streak), doctrine fights ("who crucified Jesus"), invented quotes (Bible App for Kids), broken English. The DH3 reviews add that missed answers never show the right one.
- **Ads kill trivia apps.** Indecent ads and scam ads inside a Bible game get the angriest reviews of all.
- **Lost progress hurts the most loyal users.** Shepherd ("33 day streak"), Hallow, a 100+ day streak in Manna, Oakever ("lost my almost 4 MILLION points").
- **Mascots and AI split Christians.** The phoenix was called "pagan", and AI drew complaints at Hallow, Shepherd and Bible Memory. A lamb or a tree is safe. A mythological creature is not.

## 3. Ideas worth copying (ranked)

1. **Daily 5 with a share grid.** Who: Versle Classic (today's verse in 5 tries), Wordle's spoiler-free emoji grid, UniRise's daily. Ours: the same 5 questions worldwide, reset at local midnight. Share text: `Pray to Scroll · Daily 5 · #312` / `🟩🟩🟨🟩🟩 4/5` / `🔥 12 days`, with no answers in it. After the Daily 5, "Practice more" is unlimited, because Versle's cap drew 1★.
2. **A path map along the Bible's story.** Who: Manna's S-path, Shepherd's "Duolingo style Bible paths", Know The Word's Scripture Atlas, BibleBelt's belts. Ours: units = the big stories (Creation → Abraham → Exodus → David → Jesus → Acts). Nodes light up as you go. Every unit can be opened from day 1 (BibleBelt), and nothing is ever locked behind lives or pay.
3. **Lessons of about 6 cards, about 2 minutes.** Who: BibleBelt (7 questions a round), Ascend (<10 min), Manna (5–10 min), Aged Studio (3 a level, which reviewers call too few). Ours: 3 teach cards + 3 check cards, with a "next lesson" button and no forced break.
4. **Every answer shows its verse.** Who: BibleBelt ("Wrong answers get the richer explanation", with sources); DH3's 1★ is the counter-example. Ours: right or wrong, show the verse and its reference, plus a "Read it" link into the feed. A missed question comes back in tomorrow's review (Bible Reels).
5. **Five quiz types.** Who: trivia apps (multiple choice), Versle (where is it found), Bible Loop (put the words in order), Fighter Verses / VerseLocker (Word Bank, fill the blank). Ours: multiple choice, true/false, "Who said it?", fill the blank from a word bank, put 4 events in order, "Which book?". Only answers the text itself settles, in one translation (WEB).
6. **A streak that forgives.** Who: Duolingo (2 equipped freezes, +0.38% daily actives), Hallow (manual backfill), YouVersion (any Bible activity counts). Manna users beg for freezes; Ascend sells Streak Shields. Ours: any lesson, Daily 5 or feed card counts. A free grace day refills weekly and is applied automatically. Copy: "You were away. Grace covered it." Never sold.
7. **XP that only adds.** Who: Shepherd / Ascend (XP + gems), Duolingo (XP drives leagues). Ours: XP for lessons and Daily 5 that is never spent and never lost. No hearts, no energy, no gems store.
8. **Celebrations at milestones.** Who: Duolingo (milestone animations +1.7% D7 retention; a 7-day streak = "3.6 times more likely to complete their course"), YouVersion's 7-day mini celebrations, BibleProject's class certificate. Ours: a short burst at each lesson end, a full-screen moment at day 3/7/30/100, and a shareable "Finished: The Exodus" card when a unit is done.
9. **Memorizing verses with first letters and spaced review.** Who: Bible Memory App (first-letter typing, a "forgiving keyboard"), Verses (Absorb → Memorize → Recall), Remember Me (progressive first-letter hints, spaced repetition, free), Fighter Verses (graduated intervals). Ours: one memory verse per unit in 3 steps (read → blanks → first letters). Reviews come back at 1, 3, 7, 21 and 60 days, and memorized verses are marked in the feed.
10. **"Where and when" context cards.** Who: Know The Word (places, a character library), Scroll The Bible Discover (Historical Context, Life in Biblical Times, Did You Know), BibleProject (an overview video per book). Ours: each lesson opens on a map pin + a rough date + "who's who". The same cards show up in Scroll the Word.
11. **Learning cards inside the feed.** Who: BibleBelt Shorts (question → answer → why it's interesting, save/share), Scroll The Bible Discover. Ours: every ~5th card in Scroll the Word is a tap-to-reveal question that earns XP, with a "Continue the lesson" link.
12. **Series hooks for the next part.** Who: BibleWatch (episodes "you'll actually come back to tomorrow"), Hallow Bible in a Year (day numbers, start any day), Pray40 / Pray25, YouVersion plans. Ours: each lesson ends on a teaser card ("Next: the sea in front, the army behind"). Seasonal runs: "Advent 25" from Nov 29, "Lent 40".
13. **A collection that fills in.** Who: Oakever Bible Word Puzzle (724,764 ratings), where levels unlock pieces of a story illustration. Ours: each lesson adds a tile to its unit's artwork. A finished unit = a complete picture to share.
14. **Social without ranks.** Who: Versle's friends ("we share our scores"), Manna's team weekly goals, Remember Me's group progress "without exposing individual scores", YouVersion's Plans With Friends. Ours: Circles see each other's Daily 5 grids and fill a shared weekly goal. No public leaderboard of strangers.
15. **Today's verse as the anchor.** Who: YouVersion (VOTD → Guided Scripture with reflection questions), Bible Loop (a daily memory game on the VOTD). Ours: Daily 5 question 5 is always "Where is today's verse found?" (Versle Classic in one tap).

## 4. Avoid

- Hearts, energy or cool-down timers: Manna's "hearts for 5 hours", Ascend's 15 energy bars, Versle's 12-hour cap.
- Paywalls or trials on Scripture or the core loop (Scroll The Bible, Bible Shorts, BibleWatch, Ascend), and ads in a faith game (Daily Bible Trivia, UniRise, DH3).
- Questions whose answer depends on tradition (canon size, "who crucified Jesus"), invented quotes, wrong references. Every answer gets a reference and a "Report this question" button.
- Guilt mascots that faint or lose hearts (Shepherd), mythological symbols (the Ascend phoenix), and visibly AI-written content.
- Gates before value: phone-number signup (Shepherd), a forced account (BibleProject), a rating prompt before first use (BibleWatch).
